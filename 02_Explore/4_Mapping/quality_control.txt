Raw Read Quality Control

I ran fastqc on two files, SRR1039508_1.fastq and SRR1039509_2.fastq.

For SRR1039508_1.fastq, there was a red flag for sequence duplication levels. Claude said this is context-dependent, and specifically is expected with RNA-seq.

For SRR1039509_2.fastq, there was a red flag for per tile sequence quality. Claude said this merits a closer look. If there are "large blocks or whole stretches of the flow cell with poor quality, that’s more serious", and we do see a patch of red/yellow around position 9. That's also where we see a small spike in per base N content to around 10% and a dip in per base sequence quality (the whiskers, not the boxes). So while most of the sequence is completely fine, that one patch might not be reliable. 

The sequence likely doesn't need to be trimmed. The per base sequence content is slightly more uneven at the beginning, but not by too much.
