I cleaned all of the transcripts by deleting content. This was either to remove failed/imperfect commands or to reduce output from commands when it became too long.

Deletions to reduce output will usually be marked with a comment, "Edited for readability/clarity" or something similar. Deletions to remove mistakes are generally not marked.
